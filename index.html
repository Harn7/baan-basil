OUR STORY

A little taste of home.

Baan Basil started with a simple idea: good Thai food should feel familiar, generous and made with care.

Our small family restaurant brings together recipes passed down through generations with fresh ingredients sourced from local markets around Bangkok.

From a quick lunch to a relaxed dinner with friends, we want every guest to leave feeling like they have shared a meal at someone's home.

The Baan Basil Family Cooking with heart since 2018
